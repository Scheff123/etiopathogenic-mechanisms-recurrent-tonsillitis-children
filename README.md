🔬 Abstract  
This article investigates the etiopathogenetic mechanisms of recurrent tonsillitis development in children attending organized groups. A survey of 83 participants revealed that 62.9% of children attend educational institutions with residual disease symptoms, and 17.1% exhibit significant illness signs. Overcrowded group conditions (54.1% with 15-20 children, 21.6% with more than 20 children) promote pathogen circulation, leading to a high frequency of disease recurrence. Preventive measures such as medical examinations, quarantine protocols, and sanitation practices were found to be insufficient, exacerbating the infection's persistence. A comprehensive strategy to improve preventive healthcare and reorganize educational processes is proposed.
